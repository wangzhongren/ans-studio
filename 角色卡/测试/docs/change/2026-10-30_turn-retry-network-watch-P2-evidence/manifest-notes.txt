Manifest format and verification (turn-retry-network-watch P2, F1 rework)
======================================================================

- `sha256.txt` lists every evidence file except itself in `sha256sum` format
  (`<sha256> *<path>`).
- Generation/verification口径 (EOL):
  * Text files (`*.txt`, `*.log`, `*.json`, `*.sha256`, `*.sh`) are normalized to
    LF (CR stripped) before hashing. PNG screenshots are hashed raw (binary).
  * In THIS worktree the files are stored LF as generated, so plain
    `sha256sum -c sha256.txt` passes (run from this directory).
  * On a fresh checkout, Git (`core.autocrlf=true`) may rewrite tracked text
    files to CRLF. Use `bash verify-sha256-normalized.sh` — it strips CR from
    both manifest lines and text content before hashing, and hashes `.png`
    raw. This is the reproducible cross-checkout check.
- Raw logs and PNG screenshots are gitignored (`.gitignore`: `*.log`,
  `角色卡/**/docs/**/*.png`); they remain on disk in this evidence directory
  and are fixed by these hashes. Committed files are the summaries, reports,
  candidate hashes and this manifest.
